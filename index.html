About

I started Ascend Performance Marketing in January 2026 at 21. We are a door to door sales company that signs local families up for community solar. Subscribers get solar credits on their electric bill and pay less for those credits than they are worth, with no panels and nothing to install. The power comes from solar farms built right in their community.

Today we have 40 reps on four producing teams, three in Albuquerque and one in Colorado. We sign up subscribers for SunShare and US Solar, serving PNM customers in New Mexico and Xcel Energy customers in Colorado. Our best day so far was 800,000 kilowatt hours booked in a single day. I spend most of my time recruiting, training and leading the team, and I still knock doors.

I grew up in Knoxville, Tennessee, went to the Webb School of Knoxville, and played Division I soccer at East Tennessee State University. Building a sales floor turned out to demand the same things as a locker room: standards, coachability, and people who show up every day.
