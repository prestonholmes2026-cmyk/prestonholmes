About

I started Ascend Performance Marketing in January 2026 at 21. We are a door to door sales company that signs local families up for community solar. Subscribers get solar credits on their electric bill and pay less for those credits than they are worth, with no panels and nothing to install. The power comes from solar farms built right in their community.

Today we have 45 reps on seven teams: three in Albuquerque, two in Alamogordo and two in the Denver area of Colorado. We sign up subscribers for SunShare and US Solar, serving PNM customers in New Mexico and Xcel Energy customers in Colorado. Our best week so far was 2,000,000 kilowatt hours, and our best single day was 800,000. I spend most of my time recruiting, training and leading the team, and I still knock doors.

I grew up in Knoxville, Tennessee. At the Webb School of Knoxville I played on the 2021 soccer team that went 20 and 0 and won the Division II-A state championship, then played Division I soccer at East Tennessee State University. Building a sales floor turned out to demand the same things as a locker room: standards, coachability, and people who show up every day.
